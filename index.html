About Us

The Ritual is a multi-media art experience and creative platform designed by Tom Visnes musically, conceptually and story-wise.

He has created a universe expressed through many different art forms and collaborated with Mothmeister, Vincent Castiglia and Jolie Perez, who physically crafted a world of characters and tribes.

The Ritual formed a partnership with Rain Games with the intention of creating a cinematic video game and invites YOU to be an active participant in expanding its story and realms.

For more information go to the Order ov the Seven Rays.

THIS IS NOT A CREATED UNIVERSE. IT IS A CREATING ONE.
